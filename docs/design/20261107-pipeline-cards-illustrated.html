Disassemble the song

The parts of the song, and when every word and beat happens

In
  • Song (audio)
  • Lyrics
Steps · technology
  • Split the song into stems: vocals, drums, bass and the restStem splitter (Demucs)
  • Write down the sung words with a time for each, from the vocal stem, then put the written lyrics on those times (why one method)Speech recogniser (whisper-timestamped), then our matcher
  • Find the tempo, beats and downbeats (downbeats assume 4 beats a bar)Audio analysis library (librosa)
  • Find runs of deep drum hits (for flashes of light; the directors are told where they are)Maths library (numpy), on the drum stem
Out
  • Vocal stem
  • Timed lyrics
  • Beats and downbeats
  • Drum runs

In practiceWhisper stretched the first word after a silence back over the gap (a line timed from 0 s instead of 10.8 s); fixed by starting such a word after the silence. Two tempo estimates are made, one from the drum stem and one from the whole mix, and they can disagree: 95.7 against 126 beats per minute for Noble Hunt.

In practiceThe directors' own universe is usually a colour grade on photographic realism (“Hand-Tinted Noir”) and is ruled out; giving the model a list of example materials made it copy the list. Putting her face onto a drawn stranger is the weaker way to draw her: 2 of 10 cases passed in our face benchmark, against 4 of 10 when the image editor draws her whole shot from the avatar still, which stage 6 does (lean only for the test shots so far). One Noble Hunt test still scored 0.06 against her photo.

Create key frames

One still per bar of every shot, checked before any video is made

In
  • Shot list
  • The chosen universe
  • Avatar still
Steps · technology
  • Draw the shots without the singer, from the description aloneImage model (MOSS-Image)
  • Draw the shots with the singer, from the avatar still and the descriptionImage editing model (Qwen-Image-Edit 2511 + Lightning speed-up)
  • Flag blank or flat stills (the checkers)Image library (OpenCV): brightness and contrast
  • Flag a stranger in the singer's shots (the checkers)Face detector (YuNet) + how light the hair around the face is
  • Flag impossible hands, people and objects (the checkers) newVision language model (Qwen3.8-27B + its vision add-on), asked questions with countable answers
  • Redraw flagged stills (blank, a stranger, or a face where only part of her should show), up to 2 more times; near-duplicates are only reportedThe same image models
  • Merge neighbouring bars of one shot into video segments of up to 3 barsLanguage model (Qwen3.8-27B)
Out
  • Keyframes: one still per barto your review
  • Storyboard sheet lean only

In practiceFace matching couldn't tell her from a dark-haired stranger (0.40 for a stranger against 0.14–0.17 for her own shots), so the stranger check uses the lightness of the hair around the face instead. Shots drawn by the image model and shots of her drawn by the image editor look like two artists; drawing every shot with the image editor halved the difference and kept her as recognisable (POC 30), but drifts from the brief's medium. The vision-model checker exists as a tool and was tested on 5 frames; it isn't wired into the pipeline.

Assemble the video

On the beat, singing cut in, colour, sound

In
  • Frames, change points, singing clips
  • Song (audio)
  • Beats, drum hits
  • Storyboard
Steps · technology
  • Put each morph on its downbeat: find the frame halfway through each change, and stretch or squeeze the time between those frames so each lands on its downbeat, taking the nearest existing frame (no new frames are made)Maths library (numpy), image library (PIL)
  • Fill the stretched stretches with in-between frames instead of repeated ones newFrame interpolation (FILM)
  • Set the colour (black and white, or keep the stills' colours) and reduce glare; on request, flashes of light on chosen drum hitsImage library (OpenCV) + maths library (numpy)
  • Encode the video and add the songVideo tool (ffmpeg)
  • Splice the singing clips in, each spanning its sung line plus a quarter-second either sideVideo tool (ffmpeg)

In practiceThe singing clips go in after the colour is set, so they are not graded; only the chosen light effects are applied over them again. A gentle pulse on the beat is on by default; it is off for every Amber video.
